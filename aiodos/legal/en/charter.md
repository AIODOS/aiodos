# AIODOS Charter

*Version 1, October 27, 2026*

AIODOS is a free and open AI workshop. It runs on your own machines and, if you want, pools
their power with your friends' machines and those of the other participants in the network.
There is no central server: the network is everyone's machines. This charter sets out the
spirit in which we use it together. It goes with the terms of use.

## 1. The spirit of AIODOS

- **Your AI belongs to you.** Your conversations, libraries and creations stay on your
  machines, unless you choose to entrust them to others.
- **Share rather than consume.** Computing power is pooled. Reciprocity is flexible: no one
  is blocked, but when a machine is busy, those who have given it the most go first.
- **Free and transparent.** The code is open (Apache 2.0 license): anyone can read it, check
  it and improve it.
- **Nothing is set in stone.** This charter will evolve with the people who keep AIODOS
  alive.

## 2. What you agree not to do

Alone or with other machines, you agree not to use AIODOS to:

- create, request, keep or share sexual content depicting a minor, real or imaginary. This
  is forbidden, without any exception;
- create the image, video or voice of a real person to deceive, humiliate or harass them, or
  to show them naked or in a sexual scene without their consent;
- harass, threaten, discriminate, or incite hatred or violence;
- design or improve malware, attack computer systems, commit fraud or impersonate someone;
- seek to build weapons capable of mass casualties (chemical, biological, radiological,
  nuclear or explosive);
- pass off made-up content as real in order to deceive: fake news, forged documents, false
  testimonies, fake evidence;
- infringe the rights of others: copyright, trademarks, privacy, personal data;
- breach the license of a model or software you download with AIODOS;
- break the law that applies to you, or to the people your use concerns.

## 3. Lending and borrowing power

- **Lending is a choice.** Your paired machines compute together from the first launch; your
  friends and the public network, only with your consent. Everything can be turned off at
  any time in Settings.
- **What others entrust to you is not yours.** When your machine computes for someone, it
  handles their request for the time of the computation, then forgets it. You agree not to
  try to read, copy or keep it, nor to modify AIODOS to do so.
- **Honest results.** You agree not to falsify the results your machine returns, not to lie
  about what it can do, and not to get around the limits and protections of AIODOS.
- **What you entrust goes to others.** A request computed elsewhere (a question, the
  description of an image, passages from a library) travels encrypted, but the machine that
  computes it can read it. Don't entrust what you wouldn't tell a stranger: keep sensitive
  work on your own machines, AIODOS lets you.
- **Receiving without ever giving** is not forbidden, but a machine that only receives sees
  a reminder: install a model, or lend its power.

## 4. Respecting others

- Behind every machine there is a person: courtesy and honesty.
- Only open libraries to consultation if you have the right to share them.
- An application you connect to AIODOS acts on your behalf: the charter also applies to what
  it does.

## 5. When the charter is not respected

- Everyone remains free to stop working with a machine: remove a friend, set a machine
  aside, leave the public network.
- The network's trust mechanisms can exclude a machine that returns false results or abuses
  others.
- Abuse or a security flaw can be reported to the project team, on its repository:
  github.com/AIODOS/aiodos.
